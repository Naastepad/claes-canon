# Canon decisions — 23 August 2026

These decisions are explicit author decisions and outrank conflicting earlier proposals, migrated representations and chat memory.

## DEC.CLAES.SPINE.2026-08-23 — CANON
Claes' long adult narrative spine is reframed.

The Brevísima / Las Casas recovery is **not** Claes' central lifelong assignment. It is his first successful reconstruction and later transmission of dangerous knowledge. The larger lifelong line is a natural-philosophical inquiry seeded by John Dee in 1564 and carried through observation, material practice, politics, war, medicine, shipping and alchemy toward Enkhuizen in 1602.

**Important structural distinction:** Dee's question in 1564 is the **seed** of the later work, but the operational/geographical spine begins when Claes **leaves Antwerp after the Beeldenstorm in 1566 and returns to Goes**. From that departure onward the Eighty Years' War repeatedly changes where he can live, whom he can trust and what his knowledge means.

The historical war must move Claes; Claes must not be placed at famous events merely to display them.

## DEC.CLAES.DEE_LIFELONG_QUESTION.2026-08-23 — CANON
In 1564 Dee gives Claes no second secret manuscript and no second cipher quest. He leaves him with an unresolved natural-philosophical question: something associated with air appears to sustain fire, animal and human life, and growth. Claes is not to name a new substance or make an anachronistic scientific discovery. He is to gather reliable observations across years.

The inquiry may involve, in period terms and without modern chemical interpretation: combustion, enclosed or spoiled air, breath, fermentation, saltpetre/nitre, plants, distillation, metals, ships, cellars, workshops and illness.

Tanneken's sensory knowledge of fermentation in `Het Gist` becomes an early unknowing observation in this longer line, not a retroactively perfect scientific lesson.

## DEC.CLAES.RETURN_GOES.2026-08-23 — CANON
Claes' return from Antwerp to Goes after the 1566 Beeldenstorm has two simultaneous motives:

1. **personal:** Cornelis, Mayken and the city that still functions as home;
2. **material/intellectual:** Goes offers the lived material world — brewing, salt, plants, tides, apothecary practice, Cornelis' craft knowledge — in which Dee's open question can be observed outside books.

Antwerp provides texts and networks; Goes provides matter, work and consequence.

## DEC.CLAES.MAYKEN.2026-08-23 — CANON
The previously open beloved/apothecary-daughter function is identified with **Mayken**, already present in the manuscript.

Mayken remains an autonomous character with her own botanical, medicinal and material competence. She is not a device who exists to heal Claes, solve his work for him or suffer solely to motivate his plot.

Explicit exclusions:
- no rape as the plot mechanism that starts Claes' northern route;
- no dissociative-identity / "multiple personalities" construction as a retrospective diagnosis;
- no requirement for a formal Goese witch trial.

## DEC.MAYKEN.REPUTATION_FALL.1578.2026-08-23 — CANON
Mayken's danger in Goes is a **slow social reinterpretation**, developing over months during the confessional destabilisation of 1578.

The same material facts acquire hostile readings: herbs, medicines, retorts, minerals, Dodoens, Claes' notes and unusual procedures can be medicine to Mayken, observations to Claes, superstition or illicit practice to others.

The escalation is cumulative rather than sudden: jokes/whispers → avoidance/lost patients → public signs of suspicion → religious or civic insinuation → a treatment followed by a death or deterioration → explicit naming/blame.

Every individual observation may be true while the collective conclusion is false. This pays off the epistemic warnings of `De Kraai`, Claes' own misreading in `Het Teken`, and the rumour mechanism that damaged Cornelis in `Het Gerucht`.

Claes leaves before certainty. His decisive growth is to understand that he can be correct and still act too late. He chooses flight before a formal conviction makes action impossible.

## DEC.CLAES.FLIGHT_NORTH.1578.2026-08-23 — CANON
The first refuge after Goes is **Gouda**. The route is not a treasure hunt and is not dictated by Dee's question. Historical pressure, relationships and livelihood determine movement; the lifelong inquiry determines what Claes notices and records along the way.

The intended macro-route after the second Goese period is:

**Goes → Gouda → Delft → Egmond/Alkmaar → Hoorn → Enkhuizen.**

Exact dates, durations, intermediate stops and occupations remain to be scene-designed unless already fixed elsewhere.

## DEC.CLAES.HISTORICAL_WITNESS.2026-08-23 — CANON
Claes is a serious historical witness, not a comic Forrest-Gump figure.

Use three distances from historical events:
- **direct participation/presence** where his location and life make this natural;
- **direct consequence** where the event changes food, work, religion, safety, travel or relationships;
- **aftermath/news/memory** where direct presence would be contrived.

He may sometimes arrive too late, hear competing accounts, or see only consequences. He must not personally know every famous figure or stand at every celebrated event.

Historical anchors discussed for the adult spine include, subject to the source layer and scene need: the Antwerp Beeldenstorm (1566), Allerheiligenvloed (1570), siege of Goes (1572), destruction/war around Reimerswaal (1573–1574), Satisfactie and confessional transition of Goes (1577–1578), Delft/Orange and 1584, the remembered war landscape of Egmond/Alkmaar, the maritime Republic around Hoorn/Enkhuizen, and the VOC/Seton resonance of 1602.

## DEC.LASCASAS.SCALE.2026-08-23 — CANON
The 1570 reconstruction remains a major epistemic climax, but Las Casas is placed on the correct scale:

- **1570:** Claes and Mayken complete the recovery/reconstruction in Goes over weeks. Claes proves that he can reconstruct hidden truth.
- **1578:** Claes returns the reconstructed/translated text to Silvius' Antwerp printing world for anonymous publication/distribution. The text is then no longer under Claes' control.

This is Claes' **first small Projectio**: truth is transmitted and can become testimony, propaganda, consolation or incitement without his permission.

The precise production/transmission route remains a separate continuity question unless closed elsewhere.

## DEC.CLAES.OWN_TRANSMUTATION.2026-08-23 — CANON_FUNCTION_OPEN_DETAILS
During the post-Delft Albedo period, before Seton's Enkhuizen appearance in 1602, Claes undergoes his own materially concrete but narratively ambiguous transmutation experience during renewed alchemical practice.

Fixed functions:
- it occurs **before** Seton's demonstration;
- it is described through matter and the senses rather than authorial confirmation;
- the text never certifies that Claes truly made gold;
- it belongs to his recovered capacity to perceive, distinguish and choose under uncertainty;
- Mayken may be present or independently aware, but her exact role is still open.

Open details include exact year, place, process, material, witnesses and what Claes himself ultimately believes happened.

## DEC.SETON.ENKHUIZEN.1602.2026-08-23 — CANON_FUNCTION
Seton is independent of the Las Casas recovery. He does not decode, authenticate or complete the Brevísima line.

The historically reported Haussen/Seton/Enkhuizen episode is used as the late mirror of Claes' already developed Great Work. Fiction may deepen the reported visit by allowing Haussen, Vanderlinden and the apothecary/medical network to connect Seton with Claes and Mayken through shared practical questions about air, enclosed spaces, fire, breath, nitre and matter.

Seton's reported projection is not the moral solution of the novel. It functions as spectacle, proof-claim and possible distraction. Claes' important recognition is that the decisive question is no longer merely **how** a transformation occurs, but **what responsibility follows if dangerous knowledge is real** and to whom, if anyone, it should be transmitted.

## DEC.HOGHELANDE.ROLE.2026-08-23 — CANON_CORRECTION
The historical Theobald van Hoghelande is too young to be Claes' older teacher. Any representation of him as Claes' formative elder master is deprecated.

His available fictional function is as a **younger correspondent / later network peer** in the 1580s–1600s, potentially linked to later alchemical correspondence and the Seton tradition. Exact meetings and correspondence remain to be historically and narratively designed.

## DEC.CLAES.VOC_RESONANCE.1602.2026-08-23 — CANON_FUNCTION
The proximity in 1602 between the reported Seton episode in Enkhuizen and the founding/octrooi context of the VOC may be used as **literary resonance only**, never as invented historical causality.

The callback to the 1542 Las Casas frame is moral and structural: empire, commerce, war, knowledge and self-justification recur under new carriers, institutions and banners. The novel must not reduce this to a simple equivalence between Spain and the Republic.

## DEC.CLAES.THREE_LEVELS.2026-08-23 — CANON
The lifelong assignment operates on three increasingly important levels:

1. **material:** what unseen principle appears to sustain fire, breath and growth?
2. **political:** what power follows when material knowledge is coupled to money, ships, war, courts or companies?
3. **moral:** does truth or effective knowledge always deserve transmission?

The third level outranks the first in the final movement.
