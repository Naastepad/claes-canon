# SRC-HIST-1542-LASCASAS-BREVISIMA-001

## Type
Historical textual provenance bundle

## Claims supported
- The critical textual tradition distinguishes a primitive version of Bartolomé de las Casas' *Brevísima relación de la destruición de las Indias* from 1542, an intermediate adaptation from 1548, and the printed princeps of 1552.
- The 1542 text closes with Las Casas' statement that he finished it in Valencia on 8 December 1542.

## Sources
1. Bartolomé de las Casas, *Brevísima relación de la destruición de las Indias*, critical edition by José Miguel Martínez Torrejón, Biblioteca Virtual Miguel de Cervantes. The editorial criteria explicitly identify the unique manuscript of the primitive 1542 version and distinguish it from the 1548 intermediate state and 1552 princeps.
   - URL: https://www.cervantesvirtual.com/obra-visor/brevsima-relacin-de-la-destruccin-de-las-indias-0/html/847e3bed-827e-4ca7-bb80-fdcde7ac955e_18.html
2. Bartolomé de las Casas, *Brevísima relación* textual closing, recording completion in Valencia on 8 December 1542. This dating is reproduced in scholarly/educational editions and follows the text's own colophon-like closing statement.
