## Triage rule

A user-supplied public-history lead is preserved, but hard Storybible claims should migrate to the strongest available source. The chain is:

`web lead -> source verification -> Source Claim -> world/economic state -> possible scene use`.

No research lead creates a Story Claim merely by being vivid or useful.
