## Historical method

- Keep **original date**, **calendar style** and modern conversion separately; never retrofit Gregorian dates into a period scene without noting conversion. Some sources use Easter-style year numbering.
- Distinguish **storm**, **storm surge**, **river flood from ice/rain**, **inundation**, **permanent loss**, **fire**, and **war destruction**.
- Distinguish observer testimony, later chronicle, modern synthesis and fictional oral transmission. A later account never proves what one named character could have known when.
- Weather is historical **constraint and material process**; avoid reducing it to psychological symbolism or dramatic foreshadowing.
- Treat photographic excerpts and partially legible historical maps as locators for verification, not a digitized georeferenced atlas.
