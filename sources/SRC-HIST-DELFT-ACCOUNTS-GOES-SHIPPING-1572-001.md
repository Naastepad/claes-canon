# SRC-HIST-DELFT-ACCOUNTS-GOES-SHIPPING-1572-001 — Delft/Delfshaven vessels to Goes in 1572

- **Source ID:** `SRC-HIST-DELFT-ACCOUNTS-GOES-SHIPPING-1572-001`
- **Source type:** published transcription/edition of sixteenth-century Delft municipal accounts
- **Work:** *Rekeningen betreffende het financieel aandeel van Delft aan den vrijheidsoorlog 1572-1577*
- **Editor:** J.H. van Dijk
- **Publication:** *Bijdragen en Mededeelingen van het Historisch Genootschap*, deel 54 (1933)
- **Consulted representation:** DBNL full text
- **Primary account locator in edition:** fol. 179v, section `IV. Ander uijtgeeff van scheepvracht ende diergelijcke in den jaere 1572 gedaen`
- **Verification date:** 2026-08-30

## Evidence

The 1572 shipping-expense account states that, to carry Barthel Entes and troops from Holland to Goes, various vessels lying both before Delft and at Delfshaven were requisitioned/arrested. Twenty-one vessels were subsequently allowed 42 guilders each by the States because they had also been brought/held before Goes. The account totals the payment to those twenty-one skippers at 850 pounds after adjustments; additional skippers who had carried troops outward and back are mentioned separately.

## What this establishes

This is direct period evidence that vessels drawn from the **Delft/Delfshaven shipping pool physically operated to Goes in 1572**.

For Claes this materially strengthens the corridor:

`Delft / Delfshaven -> Zeeland / Goes`

The route no longer depends only on later 1608-1609 Goes-Delfshaven comparators.

## Boundaries

- This is a **military requisition/transport account**, not a normal commercial freight contract.
- The 42-guilder allowance must not be used as De Hont's freight rate or as a general Delft-Goes tariff.
- The entry does not identify vessel types, tonnage, cargo capacity, sailing duration or exact watercourse used by each vessel.
- It does not identify Aernouldt de Hont.
- It does not prove that one hull could move directly from a Delft brewery quay to Goes without transshipment.
