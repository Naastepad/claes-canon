## Claims supported

The consulted passages describe the medicinal weight hierarchy (ounce → drachm → scruple → grain), provide Antwerp medicinal-weight values, and document the sixteenth-century Antwerp commercial pound and subdivisions. These data allow the fictional workshop formulation to be expressed in period-compatible drachms and ounces rather than modern grams.

## Reliability / limitations

Boonman is a modern secondary synthesis drawing on historical metrology. Local weights varied by place, purpose and period; the story therefore uses the **medicinal/apothecary system** for small recipe quantities and does not treat every Antwerp pound/ounce system as interchangeable.
