# SRC-RCE-HOORNSE-HOP-CARGO-SHIP-1525-1600-001 — Sixteenth-century small cargo ship physical analogue

- **Institution:** Rijksdienst voor het Cultureel Erfgoed / MaSS
- **Page:** `https://mass.cultureelerfgoed.nl/zuiderzee-zeeslag`
- **Historical frame:** vessel construction dated 1525–1600
- **Verification date:** 2026-08-30
- **Use in Claes:** physical-scale comparator for a small sixteenth-century Dutch cargo vessel; never an identification of Aernouldt de Hont's ship.

## Evidence

RCE-sponsored underwater research in the Hoornse Hop identified wreck 30426 as a **small cargo ship** measuring approximately **13 m long by 4.5 m wide**. Constructional evidence places it in the period **1525–1600**. The wreck also preserves remains of a forward cooking area, including hearth tiles, peat, a firestone, grate fragments and cooking-pot fragments.

## Claes use

This gives a scene-safe physical comparator for the scale of a small working cargo vessel in De Hont's century:

- length on the order of low tens of metres rather than a great seagoing ship;
- broad enough for meaningful cargo and working space but still intimate in human scale;
- a functional onboard cooking area is materially plausible on at least one period small cargo vessel.

## Boundary

- The Hoornse Hop vessel is not from Delft or Zeeland and is not a route-specific analogue.
- Its dimensions do not establish De Hont's ship length, breadth, capacity, rig or crew.
- Do not infer beer-cask capacity from dimensions without a vessel-specific reconstruction.
