## Relevant evidence

The title printed on the object describes the vessel as:

`Een Heude ofte Heu groot omtrent 20 Last`

The Rijksmuseum describes it as a heude/heu and places the print in a series of Dutch vessel types used especially on inland waters.

## Project use

This is a **near-contemporary visual/capacity comparator**, not evidence for Aernouldt de Hont's actual vessel. It supports a circa-20-last heude as a reasonable authoring image for an ordinary regional cargo heude, provided the 1627 date is kept explicit.

## Guardrails

1. Do not say De Hont's historical vessel was 20 last.
2. Do not convert `last` to a universal metric-ton figure; the last was commodity- and context-sensitive.
3. Do not use the 1627 print to fix every construction detail of a vessel imagined around 1560.
4. Use it together with sixteenth-century evidence for heuden in Zeeland/Holland, not alone.
