# SRC-HIST-1563-DEE-SILVIUS-001

## Type
Historical provenance bundle

## Claim supported
John Dee is historically placeable in Willem Silvius' Antwerp environment in February 1563. The current storybible records a letter from Dee from Silvius' Antwerp house dated 16 February 1563. This makes a fictional meeting with Claes in that setting chronologically possible; it does **not** prove that such a meeting happened.

## Sources recorded in the current master storybible
- Oxford Dictionary of National Biography, entry on John Dee.
- Manuel Mertens and Steven Vanden Broecke, research on Dee and Silvius.
