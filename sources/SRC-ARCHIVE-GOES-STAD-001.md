## Relevant statement

The official archive description states that city finances were administered from **1438** by two annually elected rentmeesters. One performed the financial administration and the other supervised it. It also states that three stadsdirecteuren supervised city property and public works and had a **stadsfabriek** beneath them for day-to-day management.

## Use in the economic layer

This supports the institutional search architecture for pre-1602 public expenditure and labour payments:

- rentmeester / city-finance records;
- public-works / stadsdirecteur records;
- stadsfabriek-related payments where surviving records can be dated to the period.

## Evidence boundary

This description establishes the administrative system, not the survival of a continuous 1540-1602 wage series and not any specific wage amount.

A numeric wage requires the dated underlying payment record.
