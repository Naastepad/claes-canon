# SRC-OFFICIAL-ZH-SCHIE-DELFT-DELFSHAVEN-001 — Delft water access through the Schie

- **Source ID:** `SRC-OFFICIAL-ZH-SCHIE-DELFT-DELFSHAVEN-001`
- **Source type:** public historical infrastructure synthesis
- **Institution:** Erfgoedhuis Zuid-Holland / Geschiedenis van Zuid-Holland
- **Page:** `https://www.geschiedenisvanzuidholland.nl/verhalen/verhalen/trekvaart-op-de-schie/`
- **Historical frame:** medieval origin; direct Delft–Delfshaven water connection in place long before Claes' period
- **Verification date:** 2026-08-30
- **Use in Claes:** fixes the northern route anatomy for Delft-based shipping without inventing a complete Delft–Goes itinerary.

## Evidence

The historical overview explains that the Schie connected Delft with Rotterdam and Schiedam and that medieval branches created the Rotterdamse and Delfshavense Schie. The Delfshavense Schie gave Delft a direct connection to its own seaport at Delfshaven. The same landscape is documented by the well-known 1512 map of the three Schieën.

## Claes use

For a Delft schipper such as Aernouldt de Hont, the safe geographical starting model is:

`Delft -> Delftse Schie -> Overschie / Delfshavense Schie -> Delfshaven / Maas system`

From there, the exact channel sequence to Goes remains route- and condition-dependent and must not be stated as a single invariant itinerary without a route-specific source.

## Boundary

- This source establishes infrastructure, not De Hont's personal route.
- It does not give a Delft–Goes travel time.
- It does not establish which Maas/estuary branch a particular cargo movement used.
- Later trekvaart practice must not be back-projected into 1589 as if De Hont sailed a scheduled passenger service.
