## Canon statement
The Revision 11 timeline places Claes' first brief encounter with John Dee in **February 1563**, in or around Willem Silvius' Gulden Engel in Antwerp. Dee is not yet Claes' fixed master; the encounter plants the later relationship. The same timeline places the intensive practical formation with Dee and Silvius in **January–March 1564**.

## Epistemic status
`CANON`

## Precision note
The storybible specifies the month February 1563, not an exact day for Claes' encounter. Do not convert the historical Dee anchor of 1563-02-16 into Claes' fictional encounter date unless the author explicitly decides that exact day.

## Knowledge boundary implied
- During/after the February 1563 encounter, Claes may canonically **know Dee personally / recognize him as a person he has met**.
- This does **not** imply that Claes already possesses the later Monas, code, vitriol or security knowledge associated with the January–March 1564 intensive phase.
