## Relevant evidence

Regional research shows a major sixteenth-century hydrological change. After the storm floods of 1530/1532 the Honte/Westerschelde developed into the principal deep-water maritime approach to Antwerp. The older eastern Scheldt/Kreekrak route became shallower and more difficult, but it remained usable for suitable shipping and retained strategic/navigation relevance later in the sixteenth century.

A sixteenth-century-map discussion also records advice to Holland skippers to use the Eendracht/Scheldt route toward Antwerp, showing that navigation choices depended on channel, draft and current conditions rather than a single timeless route.
