## Claims supported

The printing dialogue distinguishes printing ink from writing ink and describes ordinary printer's ink as based on turpentine/oil/smoke-black; it describes advance moistening of paper because the printing ink is very sticky; leather-covered wool ink balls are used to distribute ink on the form; the printing medium must be sufficiently thick and sticky that it does not run from the letters; the tympan/frisket/platen workflow and two pulls for a form are described.

These passages support the historically available **printing apparatus and craft criteria** used in the Claes reconstruction. They do **not** document the fictional tannin + gum-arabic invisible typographic medium.
