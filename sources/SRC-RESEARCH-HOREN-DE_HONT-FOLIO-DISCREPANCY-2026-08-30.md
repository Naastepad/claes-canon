# SRC-RESEARCH-HOREN-DE_HONT-FOLIO-DISCREPANCY-2026-08-30 — 1589 folio locator conflict

- **Source ID:** `SRC-RESEARCH-HOREN-DE_HONT-FOLIO-DISCREPANCY-2026-08-30`
- **Purpose:** provenance guardrail for the 2 May 1589 Horen–De Hont beer agreement
- **Verification date:** 2026-08-30

## Conflict

Two secondary representations of the same Horen–De Hont episode do not agree on the RAZE volume number.

The detailed dated Levendale transcription identifies the act as:

`RAZE 2039, fol. 86, 2 May 1589`

and summarizes a `contract ende accoord van een biersteecker` between Michiel Michielsz Horen, poorter of Goes, and Aernouldt de Hont, `schipper van Delft`, under which Horen would be biersteker for beers De Hont brought to Goes, followed by conditions not reproduced in the extract.

A separate `familiepraet` secondary compilation discussing Horen gives a footnote reference to:

`RAZE 2040, fol. 86`

for the same next-year beer-contract episode.

## Project resolution

Until the original folio image or a full independent archival transcription is inspected, the project must not silently harmonize the two references.

Use:

- **working locator:** `RAZE 2039, fol. 86, 2 May 1589` — because this is the locator attached to the detailed, dated Levendale transcription of the act;
- **recorded discrepancy:** the separate `familiepraet` representation cites `RAZE 2040, fol. 86`;
- **verification status:** original folio image not yet inspected in this research pass.

This provenance conflict does **not** alter the substantive reading of the agreement. It only limits confidence in the exact RAZE volume locator.

## Contract boundary remains unchanged

The consulted extract does not reproduce the `voorwaarden`. Therefore ownership of the beer, freight payer, commission, exclusivity, risk-transfer point, leakage allowance, cask ownership/return obligations and payment timing remain OPEN.
