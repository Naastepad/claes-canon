## Supported chronology used in the Claes history layer
National synthesis supports:
- 1555 succession of Philip II as lord of the Netherlands;
- 1566 Compromise/Petition of the Nobility, hagepreken and iconoclasm;
- arrival of Alva and establishment of the Council of Troubles;
- 23 May 1568 battle of Heiligerlee and execution of Egmont and Horne;
- 1572 capture of Den Briel by the Sea Beggars;
- 1579 Union of Arras and Union of Utrecht;
- 26 July 1581 Act of Abjuration;
- 1588 consolidation of the Republic of the Seven United Netherlands.

The Zeeland regional window adds:
- 22 August 1566: iconoclasm reaches Middelburg; attacks also spread from Veere and Vlissingen over Walcheren; most other parts of Zeeland are reported as escaping the wave except Bakendorp on Zuid-Beveland;
- 1569: sixteen people executed in Middelburg in the repression after iconoclasm;
- 6 April 1572: Vlissingen joins the Revolt;
- 1572–1574: siege of Middelburg;
- 1575: Spanish movement through the Zijpe;
- 1576: siege of Zierikzee under Mondragón.

## Guardrails
- This is a public historical synthesis, useful for anchoring broad chronology. Local scene reconstruction still requires specialist or archival evidence.
- The statement that most of Zeeland escaped the 1566 iconoclasm must not be converted into a claim that no local religious tension existed in Goes or Reimerswaal.
