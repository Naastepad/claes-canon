- **Use in Claes:** establishes preservation/provenance of the underlying Levendale protocol family and clarifies the boundary between the archival originals and the public Harthoorn/Smallegange transcription/index layer.

## Evidence

Erfgoed van Goes states that five protocols of notary Petrus Levendale survive for the period 1576–1606 and are held under **RAZE 2038–2042**. It separately explains that Paul Harthoorn transcribed or indexed these protocols and that this material was subsequently published online by Chiel Smallegange.

This distinction matters for the 2 May 1589 Horen–De Hont agreement. The public Harthoorn/Smallegange extract identifies the primary locator as `RAZE 2039, fol. 86` and summarizes the arrangement but omits the conditions. The official archive description establishes that the protocol family itself survives; therefore the omitted conditions are a **primary-folio retrieval target**, not evidence that the original act is lost.

## Claes use

- Keep ownership, commission, freight rate, exclusivity, cask-return terms and cargo-risk allocation **OPEN** until fol. 86 itself is inspected.
- Do not reconstruct omitted contractual clauses from later custom.
- Treat a future inspection or archival reproduction of `RAZE 2039, fol. 86` as an evidence-upgrade event.

## Boundary

The official web page does not reproduce fol. 86 or its full contract text. Preservation of the protocol does not by itself establish what the omitted conditions say.
