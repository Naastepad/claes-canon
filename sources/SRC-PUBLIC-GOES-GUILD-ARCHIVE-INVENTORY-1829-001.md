## Relevant inventory evidence

The page reports that the inventory of the Goese guild archives included, for the **timmerliedengilde**:

- a book of ordinances concerning timmerlieden, kuipers, stoeldraaiers, wannemakers, mandenmakers, wagenmakers and metselaars;
- **rekeningboeken 1547–1798**;
- a notulenboek 1754–1795;
- **resoluties 1586–1798**.

The same inventory reports several other guild account series beginning in the sixteenth century, including kramers (1546), schoen-/gareelmakers (1546), wevers/hoedenmakers (1547) and kleermakers (1555).

## Use in the economic layer

The timmerliedengilde account books provide a demonstrably pre-1602 **local source route** for craft-economic research beginning within Claes' childhood.

## Evidence boundary

The inventory proves that account books survived with this date span. It does **not** prove that they contain day-wage entries or a continuous wage series. Individual entries must be inspected and classified before any numerical wage is promoted.
