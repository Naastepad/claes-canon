# SRC-HIST-GUICCIARDINI-DELFT-BEER-ZEELAND-1612-001 — Delft beer exported to Zeeland

- **Source ID:** `SRC-HIST-GUICCIARDINI-DELFT-BEER-ZEELAND-1612-001`
- **Source type:** early-modern descriptive source, Dutch edition with later editorial additions
- **Work:** Lodovico Guicciardini, *Beschrijvinghe van alle de Neder-landen*, Dutch translation by C. Kiliaan, edition expanded by Petrus Montanus
- **Consulted representation:** DBNL diplomatic text of the 1979 facsimile of the 1612 edition
- **Publication attestation used:** 1612
- **Original Italian work:** 1567
- **Verification date:** 2026-08-30

## Evidence

In the Delft description, the running text states that Delft brewed much good beer which was carried **particularly to Zeeland** and onward to other towns.

A marked `Byv.` addition expands the picture: Delft beer was carried to Flanders, Holland, Zeeland, Overijssel and Friesland, and in those regions Delft had its `Biersteeckers`. It also describes many foreign skippers waiting in Delft for beer to be brewed and emphasizes the brewery economy's dependence on casks, carriers, merchants, inns and credit.

## Chronology boundary

DBNL identifies the consulted text as the 1612 Dutch edition and notes that the original Italian work dates to 1567 but that the Dutch edition was translated and expanded, including additions by Petrus Montanus. Therefore:

- the **1612 edition securely attests** the Delft-to-Zeeland beer-distribution mechanism;
- the exact textual layer of every sentence must not be silently dated to 1567 without edition comparison;
- the `Byv.` passage about regional `Biersteeckers` is treated here as a 1612 attestation, not as proven 1567 wording.

## Claes use

This source strongly supports the commercial plausibility of a Delft/Holland beer flow into Zeeland and a local biersteker layer. It does not prove De Hont's specific cargo origin, brewer, ship, route, schedule or contractual role in 1589.
