# SRC-HIST-GOES-NIEUWSTRAAT-001 — pre-1594 Nieuwstraat / Armenhoek

**Type:** composite historical/toponymic evidence bundle  
**Status:** research provenance  
**Scope:** distinction between the deed-name `Nieuwstraat` before 1594 and the planmatige Nieuwstraat laid out from 1594.

## Primary transport evidence

The RAZE transport corpus used by the Goes Stadsatlas contains `Nieuwstraat` references before 1594. The key Nissepat act is:

- `RAZE-1746-0153`, 20 March 1542: Huygh Olielampe sells Claes Jacopsen Nissepat a house in the `Nieuwstraat`; the street is the eastern boundary and Jacob Dierixsen de Bye borders south, west and north. The parcel is therefore on the west side of that historical street/toponym.

The wider corpus records `Nieuwstraat` before 1542 (at least 1537/1538 in the normalized dataset) and later contains the explicit forms `Armenhoek in de Nieuwstraat`, `Oude Nieuwstraat`, and `Oude Nieuwstraat bij de Armenhoek`. These later locators support continuity of an older Nieuwstraat-name in or by the Armenhoek, while not proving an exact sixteenth-century street axis.

## Urban-development control

Zeeuwse Ankers, **Goes**, states that the first expansion plan of Goes dates to 1594, when the city fathers decided to lay out the Nieuwstraat. This is evidence for the planned/current Nieuwstraat as a late-sixteenth-century urban extension, not evidence that no earlier, different place-name `Nieuwstraat` existed.

Source: https://www.zeeuwseankers.nl/verhaal/goes

## Reconstruction decision boundary

The evidence supports three different certainty levels:

1. **Toponym existence before 1594 — VERIFIED:** transport acts explicitly use `Nieuwstraat`.
2. **Identity with the Armenhoek/Oude Nieuwstraat cluster — SUPPORTED:** later transport locators explicitly connect Nieuwstraat and Armenhoek and use `Oude Nieuwstraat`.
3. **Exact street axis in 1542 — UNKNOWN:** no source currently fixes a cadastral-quality line.

For story/atlas use, `ENT.LOC.GOES.NIEUWSTRAAT_PRE1594` therefore represents the older Nieuwstraat/Oude Nieuwstraat in or by the Armenhoek. It must never be snapped to `ENT.LOC.GOES.NIEUWSTRAAT_1594` merely because both contain the name Nieuwstraat.

## Claes/Nissepat consequence

The historically documented purchase of 20 March 1542 is the strongest property anchor in the exact year of Claes' birth. Making that house the residence of the fictional Cornelis household is a **story decision**, not a historical claim about the real buyer's household. The deed topology remains binding: street east; De Bye north, west and south.
