# SRC-HIST-EGMOND-ABBEY-1573-001

## Scope

Source/provenance record for the late-medieval and sixteenth-century state of the Abdij van Egmond and for the contested 1573 destruction tradition.

## Source inputs

- User-supplied prompt and transcript, 2026-08-16, including video discussion and screenshot that contrasts the conventional line "geplunderd en in brand gestoken" with the archival/reconstruction claim that the abbey was systematisch afgebroken/ontmanteld.
- Noord-Hollands Archief / Archieven.nl, archief 356, `Abdij van Egmond`, described as archive material for the abbey, with documents running into the 1570s and extensive medieval charters and accounts.
- User-supplied Noord-Hollands Archief link: `https://noord-hollandsarchief.nl/bronnen/archieven?mivast=236&mizig=210&miadt=236&miview=inv2&milang=nl&micode=356&minr=25011109`.
- User-supplied Archief Alkmaar periodical search link: `https://tijdschriften.archiefalkmaar.nl/search?query=Abdij&f_periodicalcode%5B%5D=GEE&submit=zoeken`.
- Paul Buitenhuis, "De ondergang van de abdij van Egmond in 1573", Historiek, 17 oktober 2022.
- Paul Buitenhuis / Historiek follow-up reporting the argument that Willem van Oranje probably authorized or knew of the sloop/ontmanteling through Sonoy and Alkmaar.
- Stadsarchief Amsterdam, public item `Abdij van Egmond`, preserves the conventional story that Willem van Oranje ordered the abbey burned in June 1573 to keep it from the Spaniards.
- Nationaal Archief / Grafelijkheidsrekenkamer references to abdij archival papers found and inventoried at Alkmaar in late 1573, useful as archival-context support for displacement/preservation of Egmond records.

## Reliability note

This record separates three layers:

1. the older/common fire tradition around 7 June 1573;
2. the newer reconstruction that the abbey's effective destruction was a deliberate, paid and administratively visible dismantling for Alkmaar's defence;
3. Claes story use: indirect news/context only, not direct proof that Claes visited Egmond or participated in events there.

Direct archival inspection of Alkmaar fabrieksmeesters' accounts and the exact referenced afrekeningboek remains desirable before upgrading any exact weekly-payment detail from SUPPORTED to VERIFIED.
