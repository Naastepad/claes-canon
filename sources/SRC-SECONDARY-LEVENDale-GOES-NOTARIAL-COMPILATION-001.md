## Relevant finding

The compilation identifies **Anthonis Claesz / Anthonis Claiszoon** as a Goese **stadtimmerman** in weeskamer-related material and traces him in that role/family context in 1567, 1580 and 1590. The same discussion points researchers to the Goese weeskamer notulenboeken.

This makes Anthonis Claesz a high-value **name target** for the pre-1602 municipal-account extraction: city carpenter payments, public-works warrants, acquittances, retainer/salary entries or day-work entries may be indexed or recognisable by his name.

## Evidence boundary

The consulted compilation does **not** provide a day-wage figure for Anthonis Claesz. His title `stadtimmerman` does not establish whether he was paid by annual retainer, day rate, piecework, contract, materials-plus-labour, or a mixed arrangement.

No numeric wage may be inferred from the office title alone.

## Additional local construction evidence

The same Levendale compilation contains multiple late-sixteenth-century Goese construction transactions, including:

- named Goese timmerlieden and metselaars in valuation and building disputes;
- a house-building dispute in which a metselaar undertook materials plus labour and an arbitration fixed a lump sum;
- the 1582 brewery valuation by Goese building craftsmen.

These demonstrate active local craft/contract practice but are **contract/valuation evidence**, not day-wage observations unless labour time and rate are explicitly stated.

## Guardrails
