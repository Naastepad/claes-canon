# SRC-HIST-DBNL-HOGELANDE-NNBW-001

## Identification

- **Type:** biographical reference entry
- **Repository / publication:** DBNL, *Nieuw Nederlandsch biografisch woordenboek*, Deel 6
- **Entry:** HOGELANDE (Theobald van)
- **URL:** https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/molh003nieu06_01/molh003nieu06_01_1259.php
- **Project use:** chronological correction and guardrail for Theobaldus/Theobald van Hogelande in the Claes canon.

## Extracted facts for authoring use

- Theobald van Hogelande is reported as born at Middelburg circa 1560.
- He is reported as probably dying in 1608.
- On 19 May 1580 he is reported as first enrolled at Leiden as student in medicine and liberal arts.
- His later chymical/alchemical authorship is relevant to the Middelburg/Zeeland chymical reputation line.

## Canon implication

The source supports Theobaldus/Hogelande as a genuine Zeeland/Middelburg chymical figure for the novel world, but it makes him chronologically unsuitable as Claes' early alchemical teacher during the 1550s or early 1560s.

Allowed fictional uses:

- later reputation figure;
- later contact, comparator, younger counterpart or echo;
- evidence that Middelburg can carry a chymical/intellectual resonance.

Blocked without further explicit author decision:

- Theobaldus as Claes' childhood or pre-1570 master;
- Theobaldus as the cause of Claes' early Middelburg schooling;
- back-projecting his later printed reputation into Claes' youth.
