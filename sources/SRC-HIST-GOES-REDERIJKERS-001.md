# SRC-HIST-GOES-REDERIJKERS-001 — Goese rederijkers and meeting places

**Type:** historical institutional/topographic evidence bundle  
**Status:** research provenance  
**Scope:** Goese rederijkerskamers, especially the meeting environment relevant to Cornelis' mid-sixteenth-century story period.

## Core finding

The proposition that the Goese rederijkers already met in Cornelis' period in the Sint-Sebastiaanshof is not supported by the strongest available sources. The chronological sequence is instead:

- the **Nardusbloem met in the Zusterhuis until 1626**;
- in **1626** it moved to the building/hof of the Sint-Sebastiaangilde (handbow guild).

The Koninklijk Zeeuwsch Genootschap der Wetenschappen states this sequence explicitly. P.J. Meertens independently gives the same institutional chronology: the rhetoricians initially had a meeting room in the Zusterhuis and moved to the handbow hof when the orphanage displaced them in 1626.

## The Zusterhuis as place

Erfgoed van Goes documents the former convent of the Zwarte Zusters at the **Singelstraat**, the complex later converted into the orphanage. The orphanage plans became concrete in 1626 and the institution opened there on 1 January 1628. This is spatially consistent with the Zusterhuis from which the Nardusbloem was displaced.

Sources:
- https://erfgoedvangoes.nl/weeshuis/
- https://collecties.erfgoedvangoes.nl/detail.php?id=1833&index=8&nav_id=0-1

For scene blocking, `ENT.LOC.GOES.ZUSTERHUIS` therefore denotes the Zusterhuis/former Zwarte-Zusters complex at the Singelstraat. The exact interior room used by the rhetoricians is not fixed by the current evidence.

## Chambers and dates

The KZGW Goes repertory and Erfgoed van Goes distinguish four named chambers in the Goese record:

- **De Goudtsblome** — documented from 11 March 1540 to 10 May 1543 in the KZGW repertory;
- **De Clisblome** — documented in 1545/1546, apparently briefly;
- **De Nardusbloem** — the name/devise is attested in the mid-sixteenth century, while the older Goese rhetorician tradition and `camer van Magdalena` precede that name;
- **De Edele Castanienbloem** — first documented in 1595 and merged into the Nardusbloem in 1630.

## Guardrails

- The **Zusterhuis evidence is specifically secure for the Nardusbloem/old Goese chamber**; do not silently assert that every separately named Goese chamber used the same room.
- The Sint-Sebastiaanshof belongs to the **post-1626** meeting chronology and must not be back-projected into Cornelis' 1540s–1560s scenes.
- The Maria Magdalenakerk had devotional and institutional importance for the Nardusbloem, but a chapel or devotional link is not the same relation as the worldly meeting room.
- The fixed meeting place of the Edele Castanienbloem before its 1630 merger remains unproved.
- Cornelis is fictional. Assigning his rhetorician meetings to the Zusterhuis is a story decision aligned with the best-documented old chamber, not historical evidence that a real Nissepat met there.
