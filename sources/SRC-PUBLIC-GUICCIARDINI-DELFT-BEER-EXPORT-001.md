## Relevant evidence

The Delft description states that substantial quantities of good beer were transported from Delft, with Zeeland specifically named among the markets. The expanded passage also describes the large Delft brewing industry, large numbers of casks, foreign shippers waiting for beer, and a broad commercial network.

## Project use

Strong support for the general infrastructure behind the historically documented 1589 De Hont–Horen beer contract and for a believable Delft/Zeeland commercial connection.
