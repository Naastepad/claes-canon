## Relevant evidence

The source describes the **heude** as a freight carrier on the South-Holland and Zeeland waters. It also records larger, more heavily built sea-going heuden. For Dordrecht in the 1514 *Informacie* it cites three or four `geboeide heuden` of 40–50 last sailing to England and eastward, while roughly 150 heuden and ferry vessels served routes to Flanders, Zeeland and South Holland.

The source also notes sixteenth-century heuden and the use of heuden during the Dutch Revolt.

## Project use

This gives the needed **sixteenth-century regional type evidence** missing from the later Rijksmuseum print. It supports choosing an ordinary regional heude as the preferred fictional vessel family for De Hont while distinguishing it from the much larger 40–50-last sea-going variants.

## Guardrails

1. This does not identify De Hont's vessel.
2. The cited 40–50-last examples are larger sea-going heuden and should not be used as Cornelis' default regional freight capacity.
3. The source is a specialist synthesis quoting older records; where exact archival wording is plot-critical, return to the underlying record.
