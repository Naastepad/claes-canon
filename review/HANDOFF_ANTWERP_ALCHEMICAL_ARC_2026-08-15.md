## Canon fixed by this pass

1. Cornelis is not merely a brewer/biersteker and not a printer. He is a Goes poorter, bierbrouwer/biersteker and deken of De Edele Castanienbloem. His role in book transport comes from the overlap of trade, vats/routes/storage, civic trust and rederijker symbolic culture.
2. Claes' Antwerp formation is organized as three visits:
   - 1561 Landjuweel: city as theatre; rederijkers, blazoens, allegory and hidden meaning.
   - 1563/1564 Dee/Silvius/Boom: city as book/workshop; kies, vitriol, hidden print and material instruction.
   - 1566 Beeldenstorm: city as broken image; symbols and texts become dangerous.
3. The 1566 Beeldenstorm is not Cornelis' death point under active canon. `DEC.CORNELIS.DEATH.1569.2026-08-15.REVISED` remains authoritative: Cornelis dies in the 19 November 1569 Antwerp execution model unless a future explicit decision supersedes it.
4. The three-visit structure may function as a hidden initiation grammar analogous to three degrees — seeing, working, carrying — but this is not diegetic freemasonry and not a historical claim.
5. The process chain is canon as a recurring multi-layer grammar:
   `kies / pyrite-bearing waste -> weathering/care/digestion -> vitriool-loog / Green Lion -> strong waters -> death/opening of Sol -> red fixation / Red Lion -> Saturn/lead/cupellation-like test`.
6. This process is a historical-chemical reconstruction and story engine, not a practical laboratory recipe and not real lead-to-gold transmutation.

## Important authoring correction

Older or draft passages that make Claes' father a typesetter/printer should be corrected. Zetletters and presswork belong to Silvius' workshop. Cornelis carries books through trust/logistics; Silvius prints; Dee opens matter; Claes learns the continuity between hidden text, hidden matter and hidden testimony.
