# Antwerp 1561–1585 — time-sliced chapter world

**Domain:** `WORLD.ANTWERP`  
**Status:** ACTIVE AUTHORING DOMAIN  
**Primary provenance:** `SRC-HIST-ANTWERP-TIMESLICES-1561-1585-001`

## Rule

Never begin an Antwerp chapter from the generic label `Antwerp`. Begin from **which Antwerp** Claes is in. Politics, public religion, trust, print risk, crowds and familiar routes change across the novel.

## Slice A — 1561: CITY AS THEATRE

### Story function
Landjuweel, multiple representation, first large-scale urban spectacle and network exposure.

### World state
- prosperous/international commercial city;
- civic/rederijker spectacle highly visible;
- fourteen official Brabantine competing chambers in the Landjuweel record;
- public performance exists under political/religious permission and limits.

### Claes access
Young visitor through Cornelis/rederijker/trade networks. He can observe, compare, remember and meet people. He is not an official Goese competitor. Dee is absent.

### Dominant scene fields
crowd movement, colours/blazons, voices/music, horse/wagon movement, market/street scale, multiple languages.

### Dramatic lesson
One sign can have many audiences and consequences.

---

## Slice B — 1563–early 1564: CITY AS BOOK / WORKSHOP

### Story function
Silvius/Dee formation; practical-humanist and material literacy.

### World state
- printing/bookselling linked to international trade;
- divided labour: setting type, presswork, correction, binding/selling and carrying;
- Latin, vernaculars and commercial correspondence coexist;
- permissions/censorship are ordinary constraints, not background trivia.

### Claes access
Copying, bookkeeping, correspondence, books/print, language study and experimental work can overlap without making him a university student.

### Dominant scene fields
paper, type, ink, press rhythm, proof sheets, voices switching language/register, account books, deliveries.

### Guardrail
Plantin may exemplify the print ecology but is not automatically part of Claes' secret network.

---

## Slice C — 1566: CITY AS BROKEN IMAGE

### Story function
Visible rupture of a previously learned religious/social sensory order.

### World state
Iconoclasm changes church surfaces, objects, routes, donor/guild memory and expectations. Public confession becomes more overtly contested.

### Claes access
He can compare remembered sensory architecture with alteration/destruction. Use `WORLD.RELIGIOUS_SPACE.SENSORY_CHURCH`; do not narrate only ideology.

### Dominant scene fields
absence where an image/object used to be; damaged surfaces; changed sightlines; altered crowd behaviour and speech.

---

## Slice D — 1567–19 November 1569: CITY AS SURVEILLANCE / REPRESSION

### Story function
Cornelis' arrest, release under conditions, renewed exposure and death.

### World state
Repression changes what can be said publicly, whose presence is noticed, how books/papers move and what a contact can safely know.

### Claes access
His knowledge is partial and embodied. He may witness changes in trust before he knows their institutional cause.

### Dominant scene fields
watching/waiting, gates/guards, documents, whispered or interrupted information, public punishment space.

### Canon boundary
Cornelis' exact prosecution/execution story is fictional; 19 November 1569 is current story canon.

---

## Slice E — 1576–1578: CITY AS WOUND / PRINT RELEASE

### Story function
Violence becomes memory; later the Brevísima becomes public text.

### World state
- Spanish Fury, 4 November 1576, changes memory and trust;
- news moves by witness, rumour, letters and print;
- print/trade infrastructure persists under political/confessional pressure.

### Claes access
In 1578 the novel uses Antwerp for Projectio of the Word. He must solve production, risk and release — not cryptography.

### Dominant scene fields
scar/repair, rumours competing with documents, workshops/books as distribution nodes, fear of ownership and traceability.

### Guardrail
The historical 1578 print world supports feasibility; it does not prove Claes' fictional Brevísima provenance.

---

## Slice F — 1585: CITY AS TRANSFORMED FORMATIVE PLACE

### Story function
Loss/change of a city that formed Claes.

### World state
After capitulation on 17 August 1585, population, public confession, commerce and print networks are reshaped by migration and new political control.

### Claes use
Do not write a simple battlefield reaction. The deeper loss is network and memory: familiar people, permissions, books, routes and assumptions no longer line up as before.

### Dominant scene fields
departure, inventories, closed/transferred shops or contacts where supported, changed speech about belonging, letters from elsewhere.

---

## Cross-slice query contract

Before every Antwerp scene answer:
1. exact/approximate date;
2. political control and current confessional pressure;
3. public versus private speech risk;
4. what print/book activity is possible and who performs which role;
5. what Claes personally knows now;
6. what material/sensory state differs from his previous Antwerp memory;
7. which information is eyewitness, rumour, letter, print or retrospective knowledge;
8. which relationship/network can plausibly give him access.

## Never write

- one timeless Antwerp ambience from 1561 to 1585;
- Dee at Landjuweel 1561;
- university-student Claes;
- Plantin as automatic conspirator;
- 1585 conditions projected into 1563;
- iconoclasm as only theological dialogue with no material/social rupture;
- Spanish Fury as a datum everyone immediately understands identically.
