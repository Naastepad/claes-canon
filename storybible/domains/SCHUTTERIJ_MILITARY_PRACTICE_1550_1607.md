# Goes schutterij and military practice — chapter-ready dossier

**Status:** ACTIVE AUTHORING DOMAIN  
**Scope:** civic shooting culture during Claes' lifetime, Goes 1572, and later drill comparison  
**Primary provenance:** `SRC-HIST-GOES-SCHUTTERIJ-DEGHEYN-001`

## Core distinction

A sixteenth-century city does not have one undifferentiated category `soldaten`. For Goes scenes distinguish:

- **schuttersgilden** — corporate weapon-practice, defence and sociability;
- **weerbare inwoners / civic watch** — broader urban defence obligation where evidenced;
- **garrison/professional troops** — soldiers under military command;
- **later standardized drill** — increasingly formal weapon sequences associated with the later war and represented famously by De Gheyn 1607.

## Local Goese guild anchors

### Edele Voetboog / Sint Joris
Crossbow tradition well established long before Claes.

### Sint Sebastiaan
Handbow guild locally attested from/around 1469.

### Edele Busse / Sint Adriaan
Firearm/kolvenier guild present in the first half of the sixteenth century. Two local heritage sources currently disagree on the start date:
- Zeeuwse Ankers: **1516**;
- Erfgoed van Goes archival introduction: **1530**.

**Authoring rule:** until archival provenance is reconciled, use `present by the first half of the sixteenth century`; do not make either date a dramatic fact.

## What a schuttersgilde can do in a scene

- weapon practice;
- maintain/organize a shooting ground or corporate space where locally supported;
- papegaai shooting/competition;
- meals, drinking and sociability;
- visible status and links to civic office;
- muster/defence participation under urban pressure.

Do not assume every social practice documented in 1616–1665 existed identically in 1550–1572.

## Body/action grammar

Military material is strongest when written as ordered bodily problems:
- stance and balance;
- keeping dangerous ends/points/fire away from others;
- loading/handling sequence where contemporaneously supportable;
- coordination with nearby men;
- waiting for signal/clear line;
- recovering after an action;
- fatigue, noise, smoke and reduced visibility.

Exact commands and formation geometry need period evidence. Avoid cinematic smoothness: early firearms and urban defence involve delay, handling constraints, smoke, uncertainty and mixed experience.

## Goes 1572 — scene model

The city is royalist, besieged and ultimately relieved. A civilian/Claes-facing scene must first decide:
- is Claes physically in Goes? (story decision/instance required);
- which gate/wall/industrial zone is relevant;
- which defenders are guild members, civic watch or professional soldiers;
- what civilians can actually see/hear versus later know;
- how food, fuel, transport, church routine and outside industries are affected.

Do not use later seventeenth-century Goese militia portraits as a costume/equipment catalogue for 1572.

## De Gheyn 1607 — what it may and may not do

The *Wapenhandelinghe* is valuable because its 117 engravings decompose roer, musket and pike handling into deliberate sequential actions. For this novel it can:
- inform a **late-world visual comparator** around the mature Mauritian military culture;
- help the author understand the concept of standardized embodied sequence;
- provide contrast with less standardized/mixed civic defence earlier in Claes' life.

It cannot:
- prove what Goese defenders did step by step in 1572;
- import 1607 equipment packages and commands backward;
- justify later `twelve apostles`, musket fork, exact fire rate or 1624 clothing in 1572.

## Rederijker/schutter overlap

Later Goese records show social/corporate intersections between rederijker and shooting-guild milieus, including Castanienbloem/Edele Busse resonance. Use this to understand overlapping urban networks, not to invent Cornelis as an officer or to prove identical membership lists in the 1560s.

## Sensory fields

### Practice ground
bowstring/weapon handling, impact at target, shouted judging, damp grass/earth, wood/metal, social watching.

### Firearm practice
smoke, flash/noise, delay/reloading attention, residue and reduced visibility. Do not add later equipment because it looks vivid.

### Siege pressure
bells/signals, distant gunfire, smoke, blocked routes, anxious information gaps and ordinary labour continuing nearby.

## Never write

- `Edele Busse founded in 1516` or `1530` as settled until conflict is reconciled;
- De Gheyn 1607 as a 1572 manual;
- twelve apostles/furket/exact one-shot-per-minute as automatic 1572 equipment/practice;
- every armed townsman as a professional soldier;
- Cornelis' schutter office/membership without a story decision;
- perfect modern drill discipline in mid-century civic defence.
