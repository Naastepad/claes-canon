# Bread, grain and baking — chapter-ready practice dossier

**Domain:** `WORLD.BREAD_GRAIN`  
**Status:** ACTIVE AUTHORING DOMAIN  
**Time window:** ca. 1540–1602  
**Primary provenance:** `SRC-HIST-BREAD-LOWCOUNTRIES-ZEELAND-001`

## Authoring purpose

Bread is not generic brown scenery. It is a transformation Claes can perceive and learn from: grain becomes flour, flour becomes dough, dough changes invisibly before the oven makes that change visible and irreversible.

Use this dossier for ordinary food scenes, a possible youth bakery scene, market/price scenes, scarcity, travel provisions and material parallels with alchemy. It does not canonize a specific bakery scene.

## Evidence ladder

### Historically supported / broadly transferable
- urban bread production can be professionally/regulatorily organized;
- grain quality and flour refinement affect price/status;
- bread assizes connect grain prices to controlled loaf price/weight;
- wheat, rye and mixed-grain breads belong to the wider Low Countries economy;
- baking requires management of fermenting dough and a heated oven rather than a modern temperature dial.

### Supported reconstruction for Goes
- default to a professional urban bakery when a scene needs an oven;
- household members may buy bread, flour or dough/services without owning a private oven;
- local scarcity and trade can change flour/grain availability.

### Open / do not hard-code
- exact baker, address and oven construction for Claes' childhood;
- exact normal Goese mix of wheat/rye/masteluin;
- exact ferment (`desem`, brewer's `gest`, or combination) on a particular day;
- price, loaf weight, proof time and oven temperature.

## Working action chain

1. **Grain arrives / is selected** — sacks, kernels, impurities, provenance and price matter.
2. **Milling and bolting** — flour fineness is a material and social distinction.
3. **Mixing** — flour, water, salt and ferment are combined; proportions belong to craft judgement, not modern recipe narration.
4. **Kneading / working** — hands read resistance, stickiness and cohesion.
5. **Fermentation** — time changes matter before the change is fully visible; smell and swelling become evidence.
6. **Shaping / resting** — division and surface tension can be shown without modern technical vocabulary.
7. **Oven firing** — wood/mutsaards heat masonry; smoke/fire must be managed before bread goes in.
8. **Oven preparation** — firing debris/ash is cleared as appropriate; the baker judges retained heat indirectly.
9. **Baking** — radiant heat changes colour, crust, smell and mass.
10. **Cooling / inspection / sale** — crust, underside, weight and crumb reveal success or failure; bread becomes commodity and food.

## Sensory grammar

Default rule: **one dominant field + one counter-sense + one inference**.

Examples:
- touch: dough pushes back differently → smell: fermentation → inference: it has changed though nothing was added now;
- heat: the oven cannot be touched/seen as a number → colour of crust → inference: craft depends on embodied judgement;
- flour dust/light → weight of finished loaf → inference: refinement and loss are economic, not merely culinary.

Do not make Claes name processes he has not learned. A child can notice before he can explain.

## Social/economic uses

- Bread quality can reveal household means without exposition.
- Bread-setting turns food into civic arithmetic: grain price, loaf weight, fraud, shortage and authority intersect.
- Scarcity should alter what appears on tables and in markets before characters discuss macroeconomics.
- Antwerp can emphasize scale, price and urban supply; Goes matter/origin; northern travel later can emphasize storage, dryness and provisioning.

## Claes links

- **sinne:** matter is read through resistance, smell, heat, colour and weight.
- **transformation:** fermentation is hidden change; oven is irreversible heat.
- **control shadow:** a skilled baker does not control every variable by force; judgement is relational and timed.
- **fire memory:** oven fire is contained productive fire, distinct from the uncontrolled Goes fire.

## Never write

- exact Celsius temperatures;
- modern recipe grams/percentages;
- automatic private household oven;
- rigid `white = rich / rye = poor` taxonomy;
- all five senses in sequence;
- a precise 1550 Goese bread assize without a local source.
