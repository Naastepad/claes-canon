## 2. Three death scripts

### Catholic civic ars moriendi

Claes begins within a civic-religious order in which punishment, confession, priestly consolation, repentance, cross, prayer and crowd compassion can remain legible inside one shared Christian death script.

### Reformation witness

Heresy executions fracture that agreement. The condemned and spectators may reverse the intended meaning through steadfastness, psalms, letters, songs, comfort, memory and later martyrology.

### Cornelis' protective silence

Cornelis belongs fully to neither script. He is not written as an ordinary repentant criminal and not as a loud Protestant martyr. His final act is refusal to give names. Silence protects others while leaving Claes without a complete explanation.
