# WORLD.GOES.CHURCH — local historical layer

Status: ACTIVE HISTORICAL WORLDBUILDING MODULE.

## Architecture and institution
- Maria Magdalenakerk is the principal parish church in Claes' formative Goes; Maria Magdalena is city patroness.
- Choir completed 1470.
- Transepts completed 1506.
- Sacristy complex added ca. 1520–1525.
- Claes knows the medieval nave; never project the post-1618 rebuilt nave backwards.
- Multiple altars/chapels were connected with urban religious and craft corporations. Exact dedications/locations require local verification per scene.

## Zeven Getijden
- 12 March 1442: first documented foundation of the seven-hours service.
- Early observance was unstable; the first arrangement did not persist successfully.
- 31 May 1471: formal re-establishment.
- In 1544 the institution is active.
- The 1471 re-establishment involved church authority, civic government, merchants, craftsmen, lesser officials and eleven guild deans.

Narrative consequence: church institution, city government, guild structure and urban identity overlap in the daily sung office.

## Music
- Organist/choirmaster documented from 1469.
- Older blockwerk organ present during Claes' childhood and in use until 1550.
- Exact location is unknown; choir placement is probable, not proven.
- Niehoff replacement organ from 1550.

## Guild materiality
Guild/corporate devotional presence can involve altar furnishings, candlesticks, textiles, vestments, liturgical vessels, books, images and insignia. Goes evidence from the 1578 transition strengthens this locally, but Antwerp's scale and exact altar map must not be imported.
