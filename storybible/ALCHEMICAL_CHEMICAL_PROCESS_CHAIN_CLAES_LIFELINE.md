## Core canon decision

The chemical/alchemical chain is not a decorative motif. It is one of the governing structures of the novel.

The steps by which Claes learns, reconstructs and eventually performs the apparent projectiepoeder/transmutation process must guide his life-line from childhood through the Enkhuizen 1602 transmutation and beyond. The material work, the book mechanism, the father-son arc, the trauma/sinne arc and the moral-spiritual arc must remain synchronized.

The governing chain is:

`kies / pyrite-bearing waste -> weathering/care/digestion -> vitriool-loog / Groene Leeuw -> strong waters -> death/opening of Sol -> red fixation / Rode Leeuw -> Saturn/lead/cupellation-like test -> projectio/release beyond possession`.

This chain is a story-canon historical-chemical reconstruction. It is not a modern recipe, not a recovered sixteenth-century laboratory protocol and not proof of real lead-to-gold transmutation. The individual craft building blocks are historically/chemically supportable; the full successful projectiepoeder sequence is a controlled authorial construction.

## Four-layer rule

Every use of the process must distinguish four layers:

1. **Historical/craft layer** — what early-modern practitioners could plausibly know or do: vitriol, gall ink, aqua fortis, gold/silver separation, cupellation, red metal preparations, practical recipe culture, secrecy and apprenticeship.
2. **Modern chemical explanation** — our behind-the-scenes interpretation: pyrite oxidation, iron sulfate, acidic leaching, iron-tannin complexes, mineral-acid logic, gold dissolution, hidden gold-bearing residue and assay visibility.
3. **Alchemical language** — what characters can think and say: kies, klatergoud, pyrites/marchasite, vitriool, Groene Leeuw, Sol, nigredo, rubedo, Rode Leeuw, Saturnus, projectiepoeder.
4. **Narrative canon** — what the story makes true for Claes: the process changes him; projectiepoeder becomes the outward form of an inward work; the 1602 Enkhuizen transmutation is materially ambiguous but spiritually decisive.

Do not collapse these layers. A source-supported technique is not automatically a story event; a story event is not a claim of scientific transmutation; alchemical language is not modern chemical explanation.

## Stage dossier

### 1. Kies / pyrite-bearing waste — rejected matter as beginning

**Early-modern language:** kies, ijzerkies, zwavelkies, klatergoud, marchasite/marcasite, pyrites/fire-stone in learned speech.  
**Modern chemical explanation:** pyrite-bearing material can oxidize under air, water and time; iron and sulfur chemistry can eventually contribute to sulfate/vitriol formation.  
**Alchemical meaning:** prima materia is not noble-looking matter but rejected matter with hidden order and fire.  
**Scene anchor:** `De kies van Boom`.

Claes must not begin with a treasure. He begins with waste: a substance workers dislike because it stains, spoils clay/brick or deceives the eye. This is essential. The alchemical path begins where social and practical judgment says: throw it away.

Narrative law:

> What looks like false gold becomes the first teacher of true discernment.

### 2. Weathering, care and digestion — matter changes by being tended

**Early-modern language:** laten trekken, verteren, rotten, uitlogen, wassen, verweren.  
**Modern chemical explanation:** exposure to oxygenated moisture transforms pyrite-bearing material; soluble iron/sulfate components can be washed out; acidic liquor can attack added iron.  
**Alchemical meaning:** putrefaction/digestion; the stone appears to decay so that its hidden virtue becomes available.  
**Scene anchor:** `De loog van Antwerpen`.

Claes learns that the work is not only heat and command. It is care. Water is added. Air is allowed. Time is endured. The material blooms, stains, smells sharp, seems to die and becomes more useful by losing its first appearance.

Use sensory writing: wet wood, sour/sharp smell, greenish bloom, blackened fingers, metallic taste as a tiny period zintuiglijke practice if used carefully, drip rhythm, patience.

Guardrail: do not supply a complete working laboratory recipe. Use workshop perception and partial apprenticeship.

### 3. Vitriool-loog / Groene Leeuw — the writing salt and the opening force

**Early-modern language:** groene vitrioel, vitriolum viride, vitriolum Martis, Groene Leeuw.  
**Modern chemical explanation:** green vitriol/iron sulfate supplies iron; with tannin/gall material it forms dark iron-tannin complexes; in acidic contexts iron can be taken into solution.  
**Alchemical meaning:** the Green Lion is the rough/green form of the Stone's power: not the completed projectiepoeder, but the force that opens, stains and reveals.  
**Scene anchors:** `De loog van Antwerpen`, memoriaal reveal, later alchemical work.

The Green Lion has three story functions:

1. it comes from rejected matter;
2. it makes writing visible by completing gall/tannin chemistry;
3. it belongs to the opening of metals and especially to the symbolic opening of Sol.

This is the bridge between chemistry and literature: the same substance-world that opens matter also reveals text. Therefore the memoriaal reveal is not a puzzle trick; it is the same alchemical law made readable.

Narrative law:

> The Green Lion reveals what was already present but incomplete.

### 4. Strong waters — corrosive discernment

**Early-modern language:** aqua fortis, sterke wateren, spiritus vitrioli, salpeter, sal ammoniac, koninklijk water as later/functional language where appropriate.  
**Modern chemical explanation:** vitriol and nitrate/chloride chemistry can be used conceptually to move toward acids capable of attacking metals; gold requires a chloride/nitrosyl/chlorine-like logic rather than ordinary aqua fortis alone.  
**Alchemical meaning:** ordinary force is insufficient for Sol; the highest metal requires a compound key.  
**Scene anchor:** `De dood van Sol`.

This stage teaches Claes hierarchy and limitation. Strong water that eats copper or silver does not automatically open gold. Sol resists until the right relation of substances is present. This allows a powerful moral parallel: force alone does not open what is highest; relation does.

Guardrail: avoid modern formula recital in prose. The story may show vessels, fumes, retreat, fear, small quantities, Dee's silence and Claes' astonishment, but should not read as an executable acid-making guide.

### 5. Death/opening of Sol — visible gold disappears without being lost

**Early-modern language:** Sol, koning, zon, dood van Sol, verslinding door de Leeuw, opening.  
**Modern chemical explanation:** gold can dissolve into a complex solution under suitable chloride/oxidizing conditions; it is not destroyed but carried in another chemical form.  
**Alchemical meaning:** death is loss of visible body, not annihilation; the noble must become hidden before it can return transformed.  
**Scene anchors:** `De dood van Sol`, Cornelis' execution as human analogue.

This is a crucial pivot for Claes. Until here, hidden meaning may still feel like a trick. Here he sees the frightening version: the most incorruptible thing can vanish and still not be gone.

This must later govern Cornelis' death. Cornelis' body is removed; his words are constrained; books burn. Yet testimony is carried elsewhere. The father becomes human Sol: not because he is perfect, but because his visible disappearance creates a hidden burden Claes must carry.

Narrative law:

> Sol must lose body before it can be carried beyond the eye.

### 6. Red fixation / Rode Leeuw — hidden Sol becomes portable force

**Early-modern language:** rubedo, Rode Leeuw, rood poeder, projectiepoeder, tinctuur.  
**Modern chemical explanation:** a red or dark matrix could plausibly contain iron oxides, cinnabar-like color language, antimony/mercury associations or hidden/reduced gold-bearing material; the story must keep the chemistry interpretable without claiming genuine transmutation.  
**Alchemical meaning:** the opened Sol becomes fixed, portable and apparently potent.  
**Scene anchor:** `De rode massa / Rode Leeuw`.

The Rode Leeuw is not merely a colored product. It is the stage where knowledge can become dangerous because it is portable. A powder can travel like a book. It can be hidden, misread, stolen, worshipped, tested, sold or protected. This repeats the book-transport logic in material form.

For Claes, this is the moral danger point: does he want proof, power, vengeance, restoration, mastery, or release?

### 7. Saturn / lead / cupellation-like test — apparent transmutation

**Early-modern language:** Saturnus, lood, projectie, toets, essay, vuurproef.  
**Modern chemical explanation:** a hidden gold-bearing powder projected into or onto lead could later yield a gold-bearing bead under cupellation/assay conditions; this proves the presence/visibility of gold, not nuclear conversion of lead into gold.  
**Alchemical meaning:** the lowest/heaviest receives the hidden sun; the dead weight of Saturn becomes the stage on which Sol reappears.  
**Scene anchor:** Enkhuizen 1602.

The 1602 event must remain double-coded:

- **to early-modern witnesses:** an apparent successful transmutation or at least a terrifyingly convincing proof;
- **to modern understanding:** hidden Sol becomes visible through process/test/assay;
- **to Claes:** the outward event is less important than the inward question: can he release the Work rather than possess it?

Narrative law:

> The proof is not that lead becomes gold; the proof is that hidden Sol returns when the whole process has prepared a vessel.

### 8. Projectio and beyond — completion is release, not ownership

**Early-modern language:** projectio, het Werk, de steen, voltooiing.  
**Modern/narrative explanation:** the public or semi-public success in 1602 is the outward completion of the projectiepoeder line, but not the end of Claes' moral arc.  
**Alchemical meaning:** the Stone is not fully possessed until it can be relinquished; the adept must become a steward rather than an owner.  
**Scene anchor:** post-1602 Enkhuizen and eventual ending.

The story should not end in the infantile fantasy of “Claes can make gold.” It should move beyond that: what does he do when he can convince others, endanger others, or corrupt himself with proof?

The real transmutation is:

`observation -> control -> loss -> responsibility -> relation -> wisdom -> release`.

## Required scene and arc integrations

### Boom

Function: Claes learns that rejected matter can carry hidden fire and order.  
Knowledge state: he does not yet know the full chain.  
Inner stage: first disciplined seeing.

### Loog of Antwerp

Function: Claes learns that process requires care, patience, waiting and partial instruction.  
Knowledge state: he begins to associate vitriol with ink and hidden visibility.  
Inner stage: working without ownership.

### Dood van Sol

Function: Claes sees visible gold disappear and learns that disappearance can be transformation.  
Knowledge state: still incomplete; Dee withholds enough that awe and fear remain.  
Inner stage: accepts that the highest thing may need to lose body.

### Cornelis and 1569

Function: the alchemical law becomes human and political. Cornelis carries dangerous meaning through beer routes, civic trust and rederijker/social network ties; he is a biersteker and logistical carrier, not a printer and not canonically deken.  
Knowledge state: Claes witnesses the cost of carrying meaning.  
Inner stage: testimony under ash.

### Memoriaal reveal

Function: the Green Lion reveals text that was already present.  
Knowledge state: Claes learns that the blank book was never blank.  
Inner stage: reading replaces decryption; revelation completes a latent relation.

### Beloved / Enkhuizen road

Function: Claes' damaged *sinne* reopens in relation, not in isolation. The beloved/apothecary-daughter line should connect bodily perception, medicinal matter, care, trust and the non-possessive use of knowledge.  
Knowledge state: Claes can work more fully because he is no longer only hiding or controlling.  
Inner stage: rubedo begins as relational wisdom.

### Enkhuizen 1602

Function: outward projectio of the material line and crisis of the inner line.  
Knowledge state: Claes knows enough to make a convincing result but must understand what the result is morally worth.  
Inner stage: projectio as release.

## Relation to the hidden three-degree structure

The Antwerp three-visit arc provides the early form:

1. 1561 — seeing hidden meaning in public signs;
2. 1563/1564 — working hidden meaning in matter;
3. 1566 — carrying hidden meaning under danger.

The full life-line extends that initiatory grammar through 1602:

1. see;
2. work;
3. suffer/carry;
4. reveal;
5. release.

This is structurally analogous to initiation, but must not be made diegetic freemasonry in the sixteenth-century story world.

## Controlling idea
