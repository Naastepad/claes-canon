# Writing Protocol — Claes

This protocol applies to any AI or human assistant asked to draft, rewrite, extend or critique prose for **Claes Nissepat**.

It is subordinate to active canon and to `AI_ONBOARDING.md`.

## 1. Writing is not canon creation by default

A prose draft is an implementation of existing canon, not permission to invent new canon.

If a scene requires a choice that is `OPEN` or `PROPOSED`, do not silently decide it. Keep the prose noncommittal or state the proposed closure separately for human approval.

## 2. Pre-draft continuity packet

Before writing, identify the smallest useful packet of records:

1. relevant `NI.*` Narrative Instance, if one exists;
2. active `STC.*` Story Claims;
3. participating `ENT.*` records;
4. relevant `OBJ.*` state biographies;
5. actor knowledge-state boundaries;
6. relevant `ARC.*`, `REL.*`, `MOTIF.*`, `THEME.*` and `VALUE.*` records;
7. relevant `WORLD.*` historical module and source claims;
8. relevant `OPEN.*` decisions;
9. Lemma result when the scene depends on a deterministic constraint.

If no `NI.*` exists, treat the requested scene as a proposal until its place, function and canon dependencies are established.

## 3. Scene contract

Every substantive scene should answer:

- Whose scene is this? POV and focal consciousness.
- What does the viewpoint character want now?
- What pressure prevents easy attainment?
- What is the opening value?
- What turns: choice, discovery, action, reversal or revelation?
- What is the closing value?
- What changes in knowledge, possession, relationship, commitment, risk, location, object state, question or obligation?
- Which arc advances?

A scene that only transfers background information should normally be revised until information is carried by desire, pressure and change.

## 4. Claes voice and cognition

Claes' core cognitive signature is `sinne`:

`sensation -> recognition -> comparison -> pattern -> understanding -> choice`

Mature form:

`observe -> distinguish -> choose -> carry`

Write his intelligence through what he notices, compares, tests, remembers and fails to notice in time. Avoid making him omniscient or a modern analyst explaining the author's architecture.

His gift is prolonged embodied observation. His danger is remaining in observation after action is required.

## 5. Do not write the Storybible into the novel

The reader must experience the architecture, not receive lectures about it.

Do not make characters explain Nigredo/Albedo/Rubedo/Projectio merely because those phases organize the book; McKee value shifts; the full recovery architecture before a character can know it; historical research caveats as exposition; or a motif's symbolic meaning.

Structure should be dramatized through event, material, perception, repetition, choice and consequence.

## 6. Historical prose discipline

Prefer concrete early-modern material reality over generalized “historical” decoration. Check sensory vocabulary, social hierarchy, travel/geography, religious practice, printing/schooling/trade/food/tools/book production and modern conceptual leakage.

Modern readability is allowed; modern worldview leakage is not.

Never turn a source-weighted reconstruction into a categorical historical assertion.

## 7. Knowledge discipline

A character may act only on information available to that character at that time. Check encounters, acquisition, understanding versus mere exposure, memory availability and knowledge asymmetry.

## 8. Object discipline

Important books, manuscripts, keys and material carriers have biographies. Verify current holder, physical state, visible/hidden features, holder belief, active function and location plausibility.

Never confuse the memoriaal's pedagogical phase with its later recovery-carrier phase.

## 9. Code and puzzle scenes

The code forces perception, interpretation, cooperation and moral decision; it is not technical spectacle.

Reader-facing merels grammar:

`shape/local identifier -> local letter/locus -> prose condition -> action/reaction -> decisive locus -> Roman theorem number`

No modern fixed coordinates in-world. P15 is not an in-world mystery. Monas does not yield plaintext. Castanea is not a leaf/fruit counting cipher. Full decode does not happen overnight.

## 10. Relationship writing

Relationship scenes should alter a relationship value or expose pressure within it. Do not reduce supporting characters to functions in Claes' development; they need their own competence, limits, pressures and choices.

## 11. Motif use

Motifs recur through changed context. Do not announce them. Reintroduce sensory/material forms and let meaning shift.

Examples: `tik-tik-tik`, incense/wax, stones versus ways, carrier/treatment/meaning, fire-air versus water-earth, old melody/changed words, blackening as revelation, bread/gest/oven, Castanea.

## 12. McKee/NOS diagnostic pass

After drafting, test whether there is meaningful value change, progressively harder conflict, a turning point arising from action/choice/discovery, exposition attached to active desire, earned outcomes, character-revealing pressure, arc/relationship/obligation movement and a useful gap between expectation and result.

External `KO.*` objects may inform diagnosis but never override Claes canon.

## 13. Draft metadata handoff

For substantial new prose, provide or record separately from the literary text:

- intended `NI.*` or proposed Narrative Instance;
- story-time window;
- POV;
- active claims used;
- knowledge/object constraints checked;
- opening -> closing value;
- arcs/relationships/motifs advanced;
- new canon choice proposed, if any;
- open questions deliberately left open.

## 14. Editing existing prose

Preserve active canon unless explicitly instructed otherwise. Identify continuity conflicts before stylistic rewriting. Preserve setup/payoff unless an approved structural change exists. Do not “improve” ambiguity into false precision. Report repairs that require canon decisions instead of silently deciding them.

## 15. Final gate

A draft is ready for human review when chronology/geography are possible, character knowledge is valid, object provenance is valid, no open decision was silently closed, the scene has dramatic function/value movement, historical reconstruction remains qualified, thematic/motif resonance arises from action/material, and proposed canon changes are separated from the prose.
